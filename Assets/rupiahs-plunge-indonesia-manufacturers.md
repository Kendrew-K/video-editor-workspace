# Rupiah's Plunge Pushes Indonesia's Manufacturers to the Edge

**Source:** https://asiatimes.com/2026/06/rupiahs-plunge-pushes-indonesias-manufacturers-to-the-edge/
**Author:** Ronny P Sasmita
**Date:** June 8–10, 2026
**Publication:** Asia Times

---

## Summary

Indonesia faces a severe economic threat as the rupiah hit a record low of 18,155 to the US dollar on June 8, 2026. The currency's record-low collapse is turning structural vulnerability into acute crisis.

## Core Problem

Manufacturing represents just 19% of GDP (down from 30% before 1997). Bank Indonesia raised interest rates to 5.25% and aggressively issued government securities to defend the currency — diverting capital away from productive lending and deepening what experts call "premature deindustrialization."

## Industrial Vulnerability

- ~70% of industrial inputs are imported
- Raw materials account for ~55% of total production costs
- When the rupiah weakens, input costs spike immediately

## Cascading Consequences

- Manufacturing expansion plans being frozen
- Labor-intensive sectors facing layoffs (13,800+ textile workers lost jobs, 2024–2026)
- Multinational corporations may relocate production
- Rising non-performing loans threatening banking stability

## Recommendations (per analyst)

- Fiscal relief for manufacturers
- Accelerated export refunds
- Expanded Local Currency Transaction arrangements
